# Design System: Dark Cinematic Editorial Portfolio

## Brand & Personality
- **Identity:** Andhyni A'syafa A-lifia (Informatics Engineering Student, Front-End & Python Developer, Event Leader & Mediator).
- **Aesthetic:** Dark Luxury Cinematic Editorial, deep obsidian canvas, royal navy statement arches, crisp high-contrast typography, and refined hairline borders.

## Colors & Tokens
- **Background Canvas:** `#080808`
- **Surface Elevated:** `#12131a`
- **Navy Arch Gradient:** `#1A365D` to `#1E40AF`
- **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Secondary Text:** `#8E929B`
- **Contrast Text / Slate:** `#CBD5E1`
- **Accent Cyan Highlight:** `#38BDF8`
- **Hairline Borders:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

## Typography
- **Headings & Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Editorial Numbers:** Playfair Display (Serif)
- **Personal Signature:** Caveat (Script)

## UI Components
- **Frosted Glass Top Bar:** Fixed backdrop blur with live status indicator.
- **Full Pill CTA Button:** Pure white background `#FFFFFF`, dark ink text `#080808`, rounded-full pill.
- **Royal Navy Arch Card:** Rounded top arch with floating corner metric cards.
- **Alternating 2-Column Work Rows:** Large serif numbered project modules.
